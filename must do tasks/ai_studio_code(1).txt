# OMNICHANNEL OSINT SEARCH PLAYBOOK FOR MERCHANT INTELLIGENCE

Use this playbook during **Phase 1 (Enrichment)** and **Phase 2 (Discovery & Expansion)** to extract authentic user opinions, reviews, and complaints across social networks and the web.

## 1. Multi-Platform Query Templates (Search Dorks)
For each merchant (`{MERCHANT_NAME}` or `{MERCHANT_DOMAIN}`), execute targeted queries across the following channels:

### Reddit
- `site:reddit.com "{MERCHANT_NAME}" (review OR experience OR legit OR scam OR shipping OR service)`
- `site:reddit.com "{MERCHANT_DOMAIN}"`

### Facebook (Public Pages, Groups & Posts)
- `site:facebook.com "{MERCHANT_NAME}" (reviews OR recommendation OR complaint OR experience)`
- `site:facebook.com/groups "{MERCHANT_NAME}"`

### X / Twitter
- `site:x.com OR site:twitter.com "{MERCHANT_NAME}" (review OR support OR order OR scam OR service)`
- `site:x.com "@{MERCHANT_HANDLE}"`

### Review Aggregators & Consumer Platforms
- `site:trustpilot.com "{MERCHANT_NAME}" OR "{MERCHANT_DOMAIN}"`
- `site:sitejabber.com "{MERCHANT_NAME}"`
- `"{MERCHANT_NAME}" "Google reviews" OR site:google.com/maps`

### Video & Visual Social Platforms
- `site:youtube.com "{MERCHANT_NAME}" review`
- `site:tiktok.com "{MERCHANT_NAME}" review OR haul OR experience`
- `site:instagram.com "{MERCHANT_NAME}"`

### Regional & Bilingual Queries (If Applicable to Repo Market)
- If the repository targets a specific region or language (e.g., MENA/Arabic or specific local markets), run parallel queries in both English and the local language:
  - Example: `"{MERCHANT_NAME}" (تجربة OR تقييم OR نصب OR رايكم OR تعامل)`

---

## 2. Merchant Discovery Queries (For Phase 2 Scaling to 700)
When discovering new merchants in the repository's niche:
1. Search Reddit megathreads and recommendation posts:
   - `site:reddit.com "best stores for {NICHE}" OR "where to buy {NICHE}" OR "trusted sellers {NICHE}"`
   - `site:reddit.com "avoid" OR "blacklist" OR "review" {NICHE} stores`
2. Search Trustpilot / Directory Categories within the target industry and region.
3. Extract competitor mentions from existing merchant review threads (users frequently compare Merchant A to Merchant B and Merchant C in comments).

---

## 3. Evidence Extraction Rules
For every discovered source:
1. Verify the source actually refers to the exact merchant (avoid homonym collisions with unrelated brands).
2. Extract:
   - Direct URL / Permalink.
   - Platform identifier (`reddit`, `facebook`, `x`, `trustpilot`, `google_reviews`, `youtube`, `tiktok`, `forum`, `other`).
   - Authentic summary or verbatim excerpt of user opinions.
   - Sentiment classification (`positive`, `negative`, `neutral`, `mixed`) or rating score as required by the repo's schema.
   - Date/Timestamp (if available).
