## Style rules
1. Clear MSA, natural Egyptian-comprehensible wording; accuracy > style.
2. Preserve numbers exactly (phone numbers, dates, amounts) — keep Western
   digits as in source.
3. URLs, IDs, evidence fingerprints: copy verbatim.
4. Preserve hedging: 'reportedly' → 'بحسب تقارير', 'allegedly' → 'بحسب الادّعاء'.
5. Preserve claim strength: 'several complaints' → 'عدد من الشكاوى' —
   NEVER 'احتيال منتشر'.
6. Do not summarize, add, or omit; translate every sentence.
7. Flag ambiguity with prefix `[AMBIGUOUS: reason]` rather than guessing.
